# Verified Pass-over Cafe customer guidance

- Customers can scan a table QR code to browse the menu and submit an order without creating an account.
- A submitted customer order is reviewed by the cashier before confirmation.
- Cash and GCash are supported payment methods. GCash payment is manually confirmed by the cashier.
- Customers may submit another order while their table session remains active.
- The assistant cannot place, change, confirm, reject, or pay for an order.
- The assistant does not have verified restaurant hours, location, ingredient, or allergen information.
